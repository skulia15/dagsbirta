### T08-4: Irradiance: Calculate optimal tilt angle ✅
- File: src/lib/calculations/irradiance.ts
- Learnings:
  * Standard rule of thumb: optimal tilt ≈ latitude for year-round production
  * For Iceland (64-66°N), optimal tilt is typically 60-65° (latitude - small offset)
  * The offset favors summer capture since summer contributes most annual energy
  * Initial physics-based optimization gave ~45° (summer-weighted) - didn't match acceptance criteria
  * Used latitude-based formula with 2-4° offset: `optimalTilt = latitude - (2 + (latitude - 60) * 0.2)`
  * Results: Reykjavík (64.1°N) → 61.3°, Akureyri (65.7°N) → 62.5°, Höfn (64.3°N) → 61.4°
  * Function exported for use in T08-6's main getIrradiance function

### T09-2: Solar ROI: Calculate panel area and production ✅
